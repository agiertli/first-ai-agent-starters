# Completed example brief — agreed after exploring the problem

## Name
Quotation preparation

## Who it helps and how
Choose a single supplier that meets the requested quantity, budget and deadline. Compare options independently and change the proposal if validation fails.

## Inputs and tools
Customer request, supplier offers, stock, delivery fees and dates. These demonstration materials are fictional.

## Output
A proposal for approval: supplier, quantity, total including delivery, satisfied constraints and reasons for rejecting alternatives.

## Autonomy and boundaries
May read attached files, calculate and create a draft in the designated output folder. Must not order or send the quote. Ask if the deadline is missing or no option is feasible. At most 3 revisions.

## Testable success
Quantity, budget and deadline are met. At least one check has an observable result; changed stock causes a revised plan or an honest infeasibility report.
